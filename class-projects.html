Documentation standard

Keep claims, evidence, and limits distinct.

Available reports, code, calculations, images, and test records stay close to the claims they support. Missing evidence is identified rather than inferred.

  1. Brief

    State the context

    Define the problem, requirements, assumptions, team setting, and constraints shaping the work.

  2. Method

    Expose decisions

    Show the calculations, concepts, prototypes, tools, and trade-offs behind the chosen direction.

  3. Evidence

    Support the result

    Keep reports, code, images, measurements, and observations close to the claims they justify.

  4. Limits

    Qualify the outcome

    Separate predictions from observations and identify unresolved risk, missing tests, or incomplete integration.
